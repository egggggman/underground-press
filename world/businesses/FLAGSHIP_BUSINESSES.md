# Flagship Businesses

**Status:** Permanent canon

These businesses are recurring Portland institutions in The Underground Press. Operational details may rotate by issue, but the facts marked below remain fixed unless changed by a later locked decision.

## The Crust Bucket

- **Type:** Pizza shop
- **Role:** Recurring advertiser
- **Canon:** Replaces Tony’s Pizza in future world references
- **Flexible issue detail:** Rotating coupons, specials, slogans, and ad concepts

Existing Tony’s Pizza design work is preserved; future references should migrate to The Crust Bucket where appropriate rather than redesigning approved page systems.

## Quality Shop

- **Type:** Neighborhood sandwich shop
- **Ownership:** The Shamos family
- **Role:** Neighborhood institution
- **Known for:** Outstanding Italian sandwiches
- **Flexible issue detail:** Community notices, sandwich specials, family stories, and neighborhood mentions

## Great Lost Bear

- **Type:** Restaurant and bar
- **Role:** Recurring gathering place
- **Known for:** Legendary ranch
- **Flexible issue detail:** Weekly specials, community events, overheard items, and local coverage

## Tony’s Donuts

- **Type:** Breakfast and donut shop
- **Role:** Recurring morning institution
- **Flexible issue detail:** Rotating donut specials, breakfast promotions, and early-edition jokes

## Usage rule

Do not add historically precise real-world claims. These businesses belong to the alternate Portland of the newspaper, even when inspired by or sharing flavor with real Portland institutions.
