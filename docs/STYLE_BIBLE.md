# Underground Press Style Bible

## Authority

This document is the design constitution for The Underground Press. The approved Issue #1 front page is the official visual reference and the Gold Standard for every department.

Before approving any future page, ask:

> **Would this page feel like it came off the same press as the approved front page?**

If the answer is no, revise the page. Do not lower the quality bar.

## Visual era

The newspaper aesthetic is rooted in **1991**. Pages should feel mechanically assembled, locally produced, and physically printed rather than digitally polished.

Core traits:

- warm, imperfect newsprint
- oxblood alerts and rules
- sewer-green utility fields
- mustard accents
- condensed display headlines
- readable editorial serif copy
- strong rules and modular columns
- subtle ink bleed and restrained registration drift
- handmade notes, stamps, doodles, coupons, and classifieds used with purpose

Controlled imperfection supports the illusion; it never excuses weak hierarchy or poor readability.

## Portland identity

The paper is produced beneath Portland, Maine. Visual details may draw from waterfront industry, brick neighborhoods, working harbors, storm infrastructure, local storefronts, handbills, community boards, and neighborhood ephemera.

Use real Portland flavor as inspiration, not as a requirement for historical or geographic replication. Do not invent claims that imply real-world historical accuracy. The setting is an alternate Portland in a TMNT-inspired world.

## Editorial identity

The Underground Press is not merely “a TMNT newspaper.” It is:

> **A neighborhood newspaper documenting life beneath Portland, Maine in a TMNT-inspired world.**

Pages should celebrate neighborhood businesses, community events, puzzles, local game stores, everyday heroes, small traditions, humor, and continuity.

## Page approval standard

A page is ready only when it:

1. belongs visibly to the same publication as the approved Issue #1 front page;
2. uses hierarchy, typography, color, texture, and spacing intentionally;
3. carries a primary story, a secondary story, and a discovery;
4. feels rooted in 1991 without becoming illegible or costume-like;
5. reflects the Portland world where appropriate;
6. preserves approved content systems and live data;
7. meets the existing quality bar rather than averaging down to unfinished pages.

## Preservation rule

Do not redesign the approved:

- Front Page
- Puzzle Dojo
- Editorial Template
- City Beat Template
- Tournament Report
- Component Library

Only migrate world references where appropriate. Preserve puzzle and game data exactly when applying visual templates.
