# Canon

## Purpose

This register summarizes immutable project truths. The detailed World Bible and locked decisions remain authoritative. Additions become locked only through explicit approval; drafts, rumors, legends, advertisements, and memories do not silently rewrite canon.

## Immutable canon

1. **Portland, Maine is the official setting.** The Underground Press is published beneath Portland in a TMNT-inspired world.
2. **The approved Issue #1 front page is the Gold Standard.** Future pages must feel as though they came from the same press.
3. **The Crust Bucket is a permanent flagship business.** It is the recurring pizza advertiser and uses rotating coupons.
4. **Quality Shop is owned by the Shamos family.** It is a neighborhood institution known for outstanding Italian sandwiches.
5. **Puzzle integrity is inviolable.** Approved puzzle templates, clues, grids, rules, solutions, and live data must remain internally consistent and may not be changed incidentally during layout or world-building work.
6. **Published issue snapshots are immutable.** Corrections are appended transparently rather than silently rewriting the record.
7. **The motto is “We’re all looking for a place to land.”**
8. **The newsroom is anonymous.** The standing credit is `Published by Nobody / Contributors Everybody / Approved by Myrtle`.
9. **Reader-facing issues follow Sliding Time.** The paper publishes biweekly, but no visible calendar year or date appears on an issue.
10. **The primary audience is Portland's underground community.** Aboveground readers discover copies organically; the paper does not reposition itself for them.

## Canon layers

- Locked facts govern setting and continuity.
- Published events extend continuity after approval.
- Shared memories are canonical as community identity and testimony, not necessarily as objective chronology.
- Legends and rumors are canonical as stories people tell; their factual claims remain unresolved unless explicitly established.
- **Canon follows publication** for non-foundational recurring features. Test ideas in issues before locking them into continuity.

## Publication framework

- The **Living Masthead** may evolve through earned issue history while remaining recognizably the same newspaper.
- The turtle-shell mark is a recurring navigation device; sewer caps are collectible issue objects. They have distinct roles.
- Special Editions are rare and earned. Each includes exactly one exclusive Artifact tied to the event or milestone.
- Almanac seasons use an alphabetical concept, but no complete set of 26 season names is canon until publication earns it.
- Community contributions and the Underground Press Club give neighbors a route into the paper without making the newsroom publicly attributable.
