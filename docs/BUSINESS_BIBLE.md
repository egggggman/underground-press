# Business Bible

## Purpose

Recurring businesses make Portland’s underground feel lived in. They may appear in reporting, neighborhood listings, advertisements, coupons, specials, overheard jokes, event coverage, and background details.

The authoritative canonical records are maintained in `world/businesses/FLAGSHIP_BUSINESSES.md`.

## Permanent flagship businesses

| Business | Role in the paper | Permanent canon |
|---|---|---|
| The Crust Bucket | Recurring pizza advertiser | Replaces Tony’s Pizza; rotating coupons |
| Quality Shop | Neighborhood institution | Owned by the Shamos family; known for outstanding Italian sandwiches |
| Great Lost Bear | Recurring restaurant/bar | Legendary ranch; weekly specials |
| Tony’s Donuts | Recurring breakfast shop | Rotating donut specials |

## Editorial rules

- These four businesses are permanent recurring institutions.
- The Crust Bucket replaces Tony’s Pizza in future world references and advertising.
- Specials, coupon values, menu details, and jokes may rotate by issue.
- Do not invent historically precise claims about real Portland.
- Recurring details must remain consistent with the World Bible and published continuity.
- Additional businesses may be added as the world grows, but flagship status requires an explicit canonical decision.
