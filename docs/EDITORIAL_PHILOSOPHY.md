# Editorial Philosophy

## Mission

The Underground Press serves the community above and below Portland with useful reporting, neighborly curiosity, and a watchful sense of humor. It records ordinary life with the same care it gives the extraordinary.

## Newsroom principles

- Report first. Label opinion, rumor, legend, advertising, and memory for what they are.
- Be locally specific without pretending fictional Portland is documentary history.
- Treat residents as neighbors, not scenery; let small businesses and everyday heroes matter.
- Correct errors plainly and preserve published issues as immutable records.
- Build continuity deliberately. A callback should reward a returning reader without excluding a new one.
- Let humor come from character, observation, and community familiarity—not cruelty.
- Protect puzzle integrity: clues, grids, rules, solutions, and live game data must agree.
- Meet the approved Issue #1 front-page Gold Standard without altering locked assets.
- **Local Angle Rule:** report the neighborhood consequences of extraordinary events rather than centering spectacle.
- **Neighborhood as protagonist:** ask how a story changes shared routines, relationships, places, and memory.
- **Stay In Our Lane:** bring the TMNT world to Maine; do not simply relocate New York plots, institutions, or story rhythms to Portland.
- Follow the underground community beyond Portland when a story naturally leads there. Editorial geography follows people and consequences, not a rigid city boundary.

## The editorial test

Every item should offer at least one of three things: something a neighbor needs to know, something the community will remember, or something an attentive reader will be glad to discover.

For a non-foundational recurring feature, also ask whether it has worked on the page. **Canon follows publication:** promising ideas are tested in issues before they become permanent obligations.

## Special Editions and artifacts

Special Editions are rare, earned records of events and milestones that change the neighborhood's shared story. They are not routine holiday issues. Every Special Edition includes one exclusive artifact connected directly to its theme.

Artifacts are keepsakes, not promotional giveaways. Each must invite readers to Display, Play, Build, Learn, Collect, or Share, and must follow the commissioning, production, QA, versioning, and archival standards in `artifacts/ARTIFACT_LIBRARY.md`.
