# Production Decisions

## D-001: Source assets are the repository record

Editable SVG, structured data, documentation, and production scripts are versioned. Proof PDFs, preview renders, screenshots, temporary exports, and transport archives are not.

## D-002: SVG is the visual master format

Reusable components remain editable vectors with live content fields written as `[[FIELD_NAME]]`. Print-only outlined copies must not replace the masters.

## D-003: Generated output stays separate

Generated catalogs, issue proofs, print exports, and previews are reproducible output. Published milestones may be archived only through an explicit editorial decision.

## D-004: Every page tells three stories

Layouts should carry a primary story, a secondary story, and a discovery element. Unused space should become meaningful editorial texture rather than stretched content.

## D-005: Controlled imperfection is part of the system

Small rotations, registration drift, paper texture, and ink variation may support the 1991 offset-print character, but never at the expense of readability.

## D-006: Licensed fonts are not stored

Font stacks are documented as tokens. Font binaries remain outside the repository and are handled during production or press handoff.

## D-007: Portland, Maine is the official setting

**Decision:** The Underground Press is officially set in Portland, Maine.

**Reason:** Portland provides a distinctive identity while preserving the TMNT-inspired underground setting.
