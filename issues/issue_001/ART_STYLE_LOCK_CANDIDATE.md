# Issue 001 Art Style Lock — Candidate

**Status:** Editor review; not locked until the representative Page 2 lead study
is approved.

The approved Page One benchmark—not the cleaner preliminary Pages 2–3
proofs—defines the physical art language for Issue 001.

## Required visual character

1. **Dominant editorial art.** Every news or department page carries one
   dominant halftone photograph or illustration with a reporting purpose.
2. **Physical press surface.** Coarse halftone dots, uneven black density,
   restrained registration drift, worn edges, and warm newsprint are visible at
   normal reading size. Texture may not reduce factual legibility.
3. **Packed modular rhythm.** Pages feel assembled by an active neighborhood
   desk. Empty space is structural and intentional, never the default result of
   underset copy.
4. **Handmade evidence.** At least two spot elements per page—pencil marks,
   clipped notes, simple diagrams, stamps, lanterns, tools, maps explicitly
   labeled illustrative, or other reported objects—must carry editorial meaning.
5. **Headline voice.** Condensed black display type, decisive line breaks, and
   selective oxblood emphasis match the force and imperfection of Page One.
6. **Department variation inside one press run.** Page architecture may change,
   but paper, ink behavior, image treatment, rules, labels, and object vocabulary
   must make every page look printed beside the canonical Page One.

## Rejection tests

A page is not Issue 001 art-locked if it merely shares the palette and grid;
looks like a clean modern infographic; uses decoration without provenance; has
large accidental empty zones; or relies on crisp digital photography without
halftone and press integration.

## Representative approval test

Approve the Page 2 lead-package study before applying this candidate to complete
Pages 2–3. Approval locks the rules above; requested changes revise this
candidate before wider production.
